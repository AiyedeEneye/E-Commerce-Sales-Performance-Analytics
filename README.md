# E-Commerce-Sales-Performance-Analytics
This dashboard is a comprehensive overview of an e-commerce company's sales performance. It enables stakeholders to monitor revenue, sales trends, customer purchasing behavior, product performance, and regional sales across different countries.

## Problem Statement

E-commerce businesses generate large volumes of sales data from different products, customers, and regions. Without a centralized reporting system, it becomes difficult to monitor sales performance, identify top-performing products, understand customer purchasing behavior, and track revenue trends across different markets. This limits management's ability to make timely, data-driven decisions that improve profitability and business growth.
This project addresses this challenge by developing an interactive Power BI dashboard that consolidates key sales metrics, enabling stakeholders to analyze sales performance, monitor trends, evaluate customer and product performance, and identify opportunities for business improvement.

---

## Business Questions

This dashboard was designed to answer key business questions such as:

- What is the total revenue generated?
- How many products were sold?
- What is the average sales value per transaction?
- Which country generates the highest sales revenue?
- Which product category contributes the most revenue?
- How have sales changed over time?
- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Who are the company's most valuable customers?
- How does sales performance vary across countries and categories?
- What trends can management use to improve future sales?

---

## Tools and Methodology

### Tools Used:

**Microsoft Excel:** Data cleaning, handling missing values and data formatting.

**Power BI:** Data modeling, DAX codes to calculate measures for key metrics, interactive dashboard development using slicers, and data visualization.

### Methodology:

**Data Cleaning:** Reviewed the dataset for inconsistencies, removed duplicate records, corrected data types, standardized text fields to prepare the dataset for analysis.

**Data Modeling:** Imported the cleaned data into Power BI, built relationships where necessary, created calculated measures using DAX for; total sales, total quantity, average sales and average quantity.

**Dashboard Development:** Developed interactive visuals including; KPI cards, charts, table and slicers for dynamic filtering.

## Key Insights

**Overall Performance**

- The company generated 249,314.65 in total sales.
- A total of 1,080 items were sold.
- The average sales value per transaction was 692.54.

**Country Performance**

- United Kingdom recorded the highest sales (86.9K), making it the strongest-performing market.
- Canada followed with 57.8K.
- United States generated 53.3K.
- India recorded the lowest sales among the four countries (51.4K).

**Monthly Sales Trend**

- Sales declined slightly from January to February.
- Revenue peaked in March (52.1K).
- Sales dropped significantly during April and May, reaching the lowest point in May.
- Performance rebounded strongly in June (51.8K).

This suggests possible seasonality or promotional campaigns influencing customer purchases.

**Product Category Performance**

- Electronics was the best-performing category, contributing approximately 30% (74K) of total sales.
- Beauty ranked second with 50K.
- Sports contributed 45K.
- Home generated 42K.
- Clothing recorded the lowest revenue (38K).

**Customer Performance**
The dashboard identifies the company's highest-value customers based on:

1. Revenue
2. Quantity purchased
3. Average sales

This enables targeted customer relationship management and loyalty initiatives.

---

## Recommendations

1. Increase investment in the UK market, where sales performance is strongest.
2. Investigate the causes of the sales decline in April and May to identify operational or seasonal factors.
3. Continue promoting Electronics, as it is the highest-performing category.
4. Develop marketing campaigns to improve sales in the Clothing category.
5. Ensure top-performing products remain well-stocked to prevent lost sales.
6. Introduce loyalty rewards or personalized offers for high-value customers to encourage repeat purchases.
7. Analyze successful strategies used in the UK and replicate them in lower-performing markets such as India and the United States.

---

## Conclusion

The dashboard provides a clear view of the company's sales performance across countries, product categories, customers, and time. It highlights that the United Kingdom and the Electronics category are the primary revenue drivers, while the sales dip in April and May presents an opportunity for further investigation. By leveraging these insights, management can optimize inventory, strengthen marketing strategies, improve customer retention, and make informed decisions that drive sustainable business growth.
